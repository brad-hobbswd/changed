CHAPTER SEVEN

Living Changed

A life changed by Jesus is not revealed only in extraordinary moments. It is revealed in the ordinary moments that make up each day.

"Faithfulness is rarely built in a single extraordinary moment. It is built one ordinary day at a time."

When many people think about living for Christ, their minds immediately turn to the extraordinary. They imagine standing before large crowds, serving in full time ministry, or accomplishing something others will remember for generations. While God certainly calls some into highly visible ministries, the greatest evidence of a changed life is usually found in places where very few people are watching.

It is revealed in the conversations we have with our families after a long and exhausting day. It is seen in the integrity we display when no one else knows the choices we have made. It is demonstrated through the kindness we offer to those who can give us nothing in return, and it is perhaps most evident in the forgiveness we extend when holding on to bitterness seems easier than letting it go.

These moments may appear ordinary, but they reveal whether the work God has begun within us is continuing to shape the way we live.

SCRIPTURE

"Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God."

1 Corinthians 10:31

Jesus never separated faith from everyday life. He did not teach His followers to honor God only during times of worship or when surrounded by other believers. He taught them to love their neighbors, care for the vulnerable, forgive those who sinned against them, and remain faithful whether anyone else noticed or not. His life demonstrated that holiness is not confined to sacred places. It is expressed wherever a surrendered heart follows God.

For many years, I believed that the greatest challenge was understanding why I often felt different from those around me. As time passed, I discovered that the greater challenge was allowing Christ to shape my responses to those differences. I could not control whether people understood me, but I could choose whether I responded with patience or frustration, humility or pride, grace or resentment.

That realization continues to challenge me every day.

DAILY WALK

Extraordinary Faith Is Built In Ordinary Moments

Most of the Christian life is not lived on a stage. It is lived in quiet moments of faithfulness that only God may see. Those moments matter because they reveal the transforming work He is accomplishing within us.

Living changed does not mean we stop facing disappointment, but it does mean disappointment no longer defines our character. It does not mean conflict disappears, but it does mean conflict no longer controls our response. It does not mean we never grow weary, but it does mean our strength is continually renewed as we place our hope in the Lord.

There are moments when obedience requires nothing more than remaining faithful in responsibilities that seem unnoticed by everyone else. A parent lovingly caring for a child, an employee refusing to compromise integrity, a friend quietly praying for someone who is hurting, or a believer choosing forgiveness instead of retaliation may never receive recognition from the world. Yet Heaven sees every act of faithfulness, and God delights in those quiet expressions of obedience.

One of the greatest dangers in the Christian life is believing that faithfulness must always be dramatic in order to be meaningful.

Scripture teaches the opposite.

David faithfully cared for sheep long before he stood before Goliath. Joseph faithfully served in prison long before he governed Egypt. The disciples faithfully followed Jesus long before they preached to thousands. God often prepares us in ordinary places before He entrusts us with greater responsibilities.

SCRIPTURE

"He that is faithful in that which is least is faithful also in much."

Luke 16:10

That truth reminds us that every day matters.

Every conversation, every decision, and every opportunity to reflect Christ matters because each one reveals the condition of our hearts. Living a changed life also requires humility, recognizing that every good thing within us is the result of God's transforming grace.

The longer we follow Jesus, the more we realize that our transformation is entirely the work of His grace. We have no reason to look down on those who have not yet experienced what Christ has done in us because we remember that we, too, were once lost. We were not rescued because we deserved mercy. We were rescued because God loved us.

HUMILITY

Grace Changes The Way We See Others

The more we understand the grace God has shown us, the more willing we become to extend that same grace to others. A changed heart does not seek superiority. It seeks to reflect the heart of Christ.

That truth changes the way we see people.

As Christ changes our hearts, we no longer view others as opponents to defeat but as souls He deeply loves. Instead of asking how we can win an argument, we begin asking how we can reflect the heart of Jesus. Rather than seeking recognition for ourselves, we look for every opportunity to glorify Him.

This does not mean we compromise truth.

Jesus never compromised truth.

He spoke with unwavering conviction, yet He also demonstrated extraordinary compassion. He confronted sin without abandoning sinners. He offered grace without ignoring holiness. As His followers, we are called to walk that same path.

There will be days when you succeed beautifully.

There will also be days when you fail.

On those difficult days, remember that your hope has never rested in your performance. It has always rested in the finished work of Jesus Christ. When you stumble, do not remain on the ground. Confess your sin, receive His forgiveness, and continue walking with Him. The enemy wants failure to convince you that you have not changed. God uses failure to remind you how deeply you still need His grace.

SCRIPTURE

"If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness."

1 John 1:9

Every new day is another invitation to surrender your heart to God, another opportunity to love more deeply, forgive more freely, trust more completely, and become a little more like Jesus.

As I reflect on my own journey, I realize that the greatest changes God has made within me were rarely sudden. They came one step at a time, one lesson at a time, and one act of obedience at a time. Looking back, I can now see His faithfulness in seasons when I once questioned His plan. The moments that seemed confusing became opportunities for growth, and the trials I wanted removed became instruments He used to shape my character.

That is often how God works.

He changes us gradually so that our dependence remains upon Him rather than upon ourselves.

REFLECTION

Faithfulness Is Built One Day At A Time

God's greatest work in our lives is often accomplished quietly. Day by day, choice by choice, He is shaping us into the likeness of His Son. Never underestimate what He can accomplish through a heart that faithfully follows Him.

Living Changed Every Day

Perhaps you are waiting for God to do something extraordinary in your life.

Do not overlook what He is doing today.

He is present in the ordinary moments of your day, in the quiet prayers no one else hears, in your faithful obedience when no one is watching, and in the unseen acts of kindness that reflect His love.

The changed life is not built upon a single dramatic experience.

It is built through thousands of ordinary moments in which we choose, again and again, to follow Jesus Christ.

One day those ordinary moments will become the testimony of a life well lived.

People may never remember every word you spoke or every accomplishment you achieved, but they will remember the love you showed, the integrity you displayed, the peace you carried through difficult seasons, and the faith that remained steady because it was rooted in Christ.

When that day comes, may your life point beyond yourself.

May it point to Jesus.

Because a life that has truly been changed never seeks its own glory.

It quietly reflects the glory of the One who changed it.
