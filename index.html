WELCOME

What Does It Mean To Be Changed?

Many people believe Christianity is simply about becoming a better version of themselves. The gospel tells a far greater story. Jesus Christ did not come merely to improve our behavior. He came to transform our hearts.

Changed explores what authentic transformation looks like through Scripture, personal reflection, and practical application. It is a journey through discipleship, sanctification, grace, forgiveness, humility, and the daily decision to follow Christ.

"When Jesus changes the root, the fruit cannot help but change."

ABOUT THE BOOK

A Life Transformed By Jesus Christ

Following Jesus is not about appearing religious. It is about becoming new. Throughout these chapters you will discover how God works through ordinary moments, difficult seasons, spiritual battles, and quiet acts of obedience to shape believers into the image of Christ.

Whether you are a new believer or have walked with Christ for many years, this book invites you to pause, examine your heart, and ask one simple question:

Has Jesus truly changed me?
