WELCOME

What Does It Mean To Be Changed?

Many people believe Christianity is simply about becoming a better version of themselves. The gospel tells a far greater story because Jesus Christ did not come merely to improve our behavior. He came to transform our hearts.

Changed explores what authentic transformation looks like through Scripture, personal reflection, and practical application. It is a journey through discipleship, sanctification, grace, forgiveness, humility, and the daily decision to follow Christ.

"When Jesus changes the root, the fruit cannot help but change."

ABOUT THE BOOK

A Life Transformed By Jesus Christ

Following Jesus is not about appearing religious. It is about becoming new.

Throughout these chapters you will discover how God works through ordinary moments, difficult seasons, spiritual battles, and quiet acts of obedience to shape believers into the image of Christ.

Whether you are a new believer or have walked with Christ for many years, this book invites you to pause, examine your heart, and ask one important question.

Has Jesus truly changed me?

SCRIPTURE

The Foundation of This Journey

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

2 Corinthians 5:17 (KJV)

Everything within these pages begins and ends with this truth. Christianity is not about self improvement. It is about becoming a new creation through Jesus Christ. Every chapter points back to His transforming power and His desire to make all things new.

MEET THE AUTHOR

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs is passionate about helping believers discover what it truly means to follow Jesus Christ. His writing encourages readers to move beyond religious routine and experience the transforming work of God's grace through everyday faithfulness.

His desire is simple: that every reader would know Jesus more deeply, walk with Him more closely, and experience the evidence of a life that has truly been changed.
