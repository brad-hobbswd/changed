CHAPTER SIX

Standing Alone

There comes a moment in the life of every believer when following Jesus becomes more than a confession of faith. It becomes a decision.

"The world may leave you standing alone, but when you stand with Christ, you are never standing by yourself."

That decision is often made quietly, without applause or recognition, and sometimes it is made in moments when no one else understands why you are choosing the path before you. It is in those moments that we discover whether our faith is rooted in convenience or in conviction.

Following Christ has never been about taking the easiest road. From the very beginning, Jesus called ordinary men and women to leave behind what was familiar and trust Him completely. Some left their occupations. Others left their reputations. Many left behind the approval of family, friends, and society because they believed that knowing Christ was worth every sacrifice.

That invitation has never changed.

The world constantly encourages us to blend in. It teaches us that acceptance is found by agreeing with the majority, avoiding conflict, and shaping our beliefs around whatever culture happens to celebrate at the moment. Jesus teaches something entirely different. He calls us to follow Him, even when that path leads in the opposite direction of the crowd.

SCRIPTURE

"Choose you this day whom ye will serve..."

Joshua 24:15

Standing alone is not something most people desire.

We were created for relationships, for community, and for fellowship with one another. There is nothing wrong with longing to be understood or wanting to belong. Those are natural desires that God Himself placed within us. The difficulty comes when our desire to belong becomes greater than our desire to obey.

Every believer eventually reaches a crossroads where those two desires collide.

Will we remain faithful if faithfulness costs us acceptance, continue speaking the truth in love when it is no longer welcomed, and choose obedience when compromise seems easier?

These questions cannot be answered by words alone.

They are answered by the lives we live.

Throughout my life, there have been many moments when I felt different. Some of those moments came simply because I experienced the world differently. At times I struggled to understand why people communicated one thing while meaning another, or why honesty was not always appreciated. There were days when I questioned whether I would ever truly fit in.

As I grew in my relationship with Jesus, I discovered something that changed the way I viewed those experiences.

God was never asking me to fit into the world. He was asking me to follow His Son.

That realization lifted an enormous burden from my heart. I no longer needed to spend my life chasing the approval of people who might never understand me. My calling was not to become acceptable to everyone. My calling was to become faithful to Christ.

Faithfulness is rarely measured in moments of comfort.

It is measured when obedience costs something.

COURAGE

Standing With Christ Is Never Standing Alone

Following Jesus may sometimes separate you from the crowd, but it will never separate you from His presence. Faithfulness is not measured by how many people stand beside you, but by your willingness to stand beside Christ.

Noah remained faithful while the world laughed at his obedience. Joseph remained faithful through betrayal and imprisonment. Daniel remained faithful when a king's decree threatened his life. The apostles remained faithful even when preaching the gospel led to persecution. Above all, Jesus remained faithful when the cross stood before Him.

Each of these servants of God reached moments when obedience separated them from the crowd. Yet none of them truly stood alone because the Lord was with them. Their confidence did not rest in the approval of people. It rested in the faithfulness of God.

SCRIPTURE

"And ye shall be hated of all men for my name's sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved."

Matthew 10:22

That same promise belongs to every believer today.

There may be seasons when people misunderstand your motives, when standing for biblical truth costs you friendships, and when your commitment to Christ leaves you feeling isolated. Even then, He remains faithful.

Do not mistake those moments for God's absence.

Sometimes the places where we feel most alone become the places where we discover His presence most deeply.

Looking back over my own journey, I can see that some of the greatest lessons God taught me came through seasons I never would have chosen. Misunderstandings humbled me. Rejection taught me where my identity truly belonged. Difficult relationships revealed attitudes within my own heart that still needed God's transforming grace.

None of those experiences were wasted.

God used every one of them to make me less dependent upon the opinions of people and more dependent upon His unchanging Word.

The Christian life is not a competition to prove that we are stronger than everyone else.

Neither is it an attempt to appear different for the sake of being noticed.

Our lives should point beyond ourselves and toward Christ. If people notice anything about us, let it be the peace we carry in difficulty, the grace we extend when we are hurt, and the hope we possess because our confidence rests in Jesus rather than in ourselves.

Standing alone does not mean isolating ourselves from others.

God created the church so believers could encourage one another, bear one another's burdens, and grow together in faith. We need Christian fellowship, wise counsel, and loving accountability. Yet there are moments when even within a crowd, you may find yourself standing for what you know is right.

Those are the holy moments when conviction becomes visible and faith moves beyond words into action.

SCRIPTURE

"I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee."

Hebrews 13:5

When Faith Requires You To Stand

If you find yourself standing there today, wondering whether anyone understands the road you are walking, remember this.

Jesus understands every step of that journey. He knows what it is like to be rejected, falsely accused, and to love those who refused to love Him in return. Yet He also knows the joy of remaining faithful to the Father's will. Because He walked that road before us, we can follow Him with confidence, knowing He will never leave us to walk it alone.

Perhaps you have spent years believing that being different was your greatest burden.

What if what you have always seen as a burden was actually part of God's purpose? What if the experiences that made you feel different were the very ones He used to teach you that your identity is not found in the acceptance of people, but in Christ alone? And what if, through it all, He was leading you to discover that true freedom is found only in Him?

The goal has never been simply to become different. The goal has always been to become more like Jesus.

When that becomes the desire of your heart, standing alone no longer feels like defeat. Instead, it becomes an expression of faith, an act of worship, and another step in the lifelong journey of being changed.
