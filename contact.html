If this book has encouraged your faith, challenged your heart, answered a question, or simply reminded you that God is still working, I would truly enjoy hearing your story. Whether your message is a testimony, a prayer request, a ministry opportunity, or simply a note of encouragement, thank you for reaching out. Every message matters because every person matters to God.

MINISTRY

Why I Welcome Every Message

Writing has never been about publishing a book or simply adding another title to my name. It has always been about pointing people toward Jesus Christ. Every email, testimony, and conversation is another reminder that God continues working in people's lives long after the final chapter has been read.

If Changed has encouraged your walk with Christ, challenged your thinking, strengthened your faith, or helped you see the Lord in a new way, I would sincerely enjoy hearing your story. Your testimony may become the encouragement someone else needs tomorrow.

I also welcome invitations for church services, Bible studies, Christian education events, podcasts, interviews, conferences, book discussions, and other ministry opportunities where the Gospel of Jesus Christ can be shared.

A PERSONAL NOTE

You Matter To God

Perhaps today you feel overlooked. Perhaps you feel misunderstood. Perhaps life has left you wondering if anyone truly sees what you are carrying.

Please remember this. God has not forgotten you. He sees every tear. He knows every struggle. He understands every question. And through Jesus Christ, He is still changing lives every single day.
