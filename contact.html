CONTACT

I'd Love To Hear From You

Thank you for taking the time to visit the Changed website. Whether you have questions about the book, would like to share your testimony, request prayer, or simply say hello, I would be honored to hear from you.

"My prayer is that every conversation points someone closer to Jesus Christ."

If the message of Changed has encouraged your walk with Christ, challenged your heart, or reminded you of God's faithfulness, I would genuinely enjoy hearing your story.

One of the greatest blessings of writing is hearing how God continues working in the lives of people from every background and every season of life. Your testimony may become the encouragement someone else needs tomorrow.

Whether your message is a question, a testimony, words of encouragement, or a ministry opportunity, thank you for taking the time to reach out.

MINISTRY

Why I Welcome Every Message

My desire has never been simply to write books. My desire is to encourage people to know Jesus Christ more deeply and to discover the life changing power of His grace. Every email, testimony, and conversation is an opportunity to see how God continues working in people's lives long after the final page has been read.

If Changed has encouraged you, challenged your faith, or reminded you of God's faithfulness, I would love to hear your story. If you have questions about Scripture, the message of the book, or the journey behind its writing, thank you for reaching out.

I also welcome invitations regarding church events, Bible studies, Christian education, book discussions, podcasts, interviews, or other ministry opportunities where the message of Jesus Christ can be shared.

A PERSONAL NOTE

You Matter To God

If today finds you feeling discouraged, misunderstood, overlooked, or wondering whether God still has a purpose for your life, I want to encourage you with this simple truth.

God has not forgotten you. He sees you. He knows you. He loves you. And through Jesus Christ, He is still changing lives every single day.
