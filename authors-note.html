AUTHOR'S NOTE

Before You Read

Every book has a beginning. Before you begin this journey through Changed, I would like to share the story behind the question that gave this book its heart.

"Sometimes God begins writing a book long before the first page is ever written."

Every book has a beginning.

Sometimes that beginning is a life changing experience. Sometimes it is a question that refuses to leave your heart. For this book, it began with a song.

Several years ago, I heard Different by Christian recording artist Micah Tyler. Its message challenged me to think beyond the circumstances I wanted God to change and instead consider the work He desired to accomplish within me. As I reflected on that message and spent time in prayer and Scripture, another word continued to fill my heart.

Changed.

That single word became much more than a title. It became a question that I could not ignore.

Has Jesus truly changed me?

Not simply my habits, my speech, or my outward appearance, but the very person I am.

The pages that follow are not based on the song itself, nor are they an adaptation of its lyrics or message. This is an original work born from my personal journey, my study of God's Word, and my desire to understand what genuine transformation through Jesus Christ truly looks like.

Throughout my life, I have known what it feels like to be different. There were seasons when I struggled to understand why I often viewed the world differently or why I felt misunderstood by others. Looking back, I realize God was using those experiences to teach me that my identity was never meant to be found in the acceptance of people. It was always meant to be found in Christ.

THE HEART OF THIS BOOK

Changed From Within

This book is not about becoming different for the sake of standing out.

It is about becoming changed through the transforming power of Jesus Christ.

My prayer is that as you read these pages, you will not simply gain new knowledge, but that you will draw closer to the One who alone can make all things new. Whether you have followed Jesus for many years or are just beginning to seek Him, I hope this book encourages you to examine your heart honestly and to embrace the ongoing work of God's grace.

If, when you reach the final page, you find yourself loving Jesus more deeply, trusting Him more fully, and desiring to become more like Him, then this book will have accomplished exactly what I prayed it would.

Welcome to the journey.

Let's discover together what it truly means to be changed.

A NEW CREATION

2 Corinthians 5:17
