AUTHOR'S NOTE

Before You Read

Before you begin this journey through Changed, I want you to understand why these pages were written, where the question behind them came from, and why the answer has become so personal to me.

"Sometimes God begins writing a book in our hearts long before the first page is ever written."

Every book has a beginning, but not every beginning starts with a blank page. Sometimes a book begins with a question that refuses to leave your heart. Sometimes it begins with a season of struggle, a moment of realization, or a conversation with God that changes the way you see yourself. For Changed, the beginning came through a combination of all those things, but one particular song helped me begin asking the question that eventually became the foundation of this book.

Several years ago, I heard Different by Christian recording artist Micah Tyler. The song challenged me to think beyond the circumstances I wanted God to change and to consider the work He desired to accomplish within me. As I reflected on its message and spent time in prayer and Scripture, I began to realize that I had been asking a much deeper question than whether my circumstances could become different.

I began asking whether I was different because Jesus was actually changing me.

That question was uncomfortable because it forced me to look beyond what I said about my faith and examine how I was actually living. It is easy to say that we love Jesus, trust Jesus, and want to follow Jesus. It is much harder to allow Him to examine our hearts, confront the things that need to change, and teach us to surrender the parts of our lives that we would rather control ourselves.

The Question That Started It All

Eventually, one question became impossible for me to ignore: Has Jesus truly changed me? I was not asking whether I had learned more Scripture, become more religious, or discovered better ways to appear like a Christian. I was asking whether Jesus had actually transformed my heart and whether that transformation could be seen in the way I lived, spoke, responded, loved, forgave, trusted, and obeyed Him.

That question became the heartbeat of this book.

The pages that follow are not an adaptation of Different, nor are they an attempt to reproduce the song's lyrics or message. Changed is my own work, born from my personal experiences, my study of Scripture, my failures and growth, and my desire to understand what genuine transformation through Jesus Christ really looks like. The song simply helped me recognize a question that God was already beginning to place within my heart.

Part of that journey has involved learning what it means to live as an autistic person in a world that does not always understand differences in the way people think, communicate, process information, or experience life. There have been times when I felt misunderstood because I did not respond to situations the way someone else expected. There have been times when I wondered whether I needed to become more like other people simply to be accepted.

Over time, however, I began to understand that my autism is not something that makes me less valuable to God. It is part of my story, but it does not define the entirety of who I am. Jesus knows me completely, including the ways I think, communicate, process, struggle, and experience the world. He does not need me to pretend to be someone else before He can work through my life.

At the same time, being different does not mean that I am finished growing. Autism does not remove my responsibility to follow Jesus, and being misunderstood does not excuse disobedience. Just like every other believer, I have attitudes that need correction, habits that need surrender, weaknesses that need His strength, and areas of my life where I still need to grow.

THE HEART OF THIS BOOK

Changed From Within

Changed is not about becoming different simply for the sake of being different. It is about allowing Jesus Christ to transform the heart while learning to faithfully live as the person He created you to be.

That distinction matters to me because I have learned that transformation is not the same thing as pretending. Jesus does not call us to create an outward appearance that hides what is happening inside. He calls us to bring our entire lives before Him and allow His grace, His Word, and His Spirit to transform us from within.

There are things about myself that I have had to surrender to Jesus, including pride, fear, selfishness, unhealthy patterns, wrong attitudes, and the desire to always be understood or accepted by other people. There are also things about myself that I have had to learn to accept rather than fight, because not everything that makes me different needs to be removed. Some things simply need to be understood, embraced, and surrendered to God as part of the life He has given me.

This is why the word changed means so much to me. I am not writing as someone who has reached the finish line. I am writing as someone who is still being changed. I am still learning to listen. I am still learning to trust. I am still learning to obey. I am still learning what it means to surrender my plans to God's plans. I am still discovering that following Jesus is not a single moment of transformation but a continual journey of becoming more like Him.

My hope is that you will recognize yourself somewhere in these pages. Perhaps you have felt different or misunderstood. Perhaps you have struggled with the difference between what you say you believe and how you actually live. Perhaps you have experienced failure and wondered whether Jesus could still use you. Perhaps you have spent years trying to become the person other people wanted you to be and have forgotten what it means to simply surrender yourself to God.

Wherever you are, I want you to know that this book was not written from a place of perfection. It was written from a place of surrender and from the realization that I need Jesus just as much today as I did when I first began following Him.

As you read, I ask you to do more than simply agree with the words on the page. Pause when something challenges you. Pray when something exposes an area of your heart. Search the Scriptures when you have questions. Allow Jesus to speak to you through His Word, and be willing to ask yourself the same question that challenged me when this journey began: Has Jesus truly changed me?

If, when you reach the final page, you find yourself loving Jesus more deeply, trusting Him more fully, desiring to obey Him more faithfully, and becoming more willing to surrender your life to Him, then these pages will have accomplished what I prayed they would.

I do not expect you to finish this book believing that you have everything figured out. I hope you finish knowing that you have a Savior who does. Jesus is still working, still calling, still correcting, still forgiving, still teaching, and still transforming those who surrender themselves to Him.

Welcome to Changed.

This is not simply a journey toward becoming different. It is a journey toward becoming more surrendered to Jesus, more honest before God, and more willing to let Him finish the work He has already begun.
