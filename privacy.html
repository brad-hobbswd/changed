PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you visit the official website for Changed by Bradley Hobbs.

Information We Collect

This website exists primarily to provide information about Changed, its message, and related resources. Personal information is collected only when you voluntarily choose to provide it through the contact form or through direct communication.

Depending on how you contact the author, the information you provide may include your name, email address, and any additional information you choose to include in your message.

How Information Is Used

Information voluntarily submitted through this website is used to respond to inquiries, communicate with visitors, respond to ministry or speaking requests, and provide appropriate assistance regarding Changed.

Personal information is not sold or rented to third parties. Information is not intentionally shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use standard browser technologies, hosting services, or analytics tools that collect limited technical information about how visitors interact with the website. This information may include browser type, device information, approximate geographic information, pages visited, referring pages, and general usage patterns.

When analytics are used, their purpose is to better understand website performance, identify technical problems, and improve the experience for visitors. Technical information collected for these purposes is not intended to personally identify individual visitors.

Third Party Services and Links

This website may contain links to websites, services, or resources operated by third parties. These external websites are not controlled by Bradley Hobbs or Changed.

Once you leave this website and visit an external website, that website's own privacy practices and policies apply. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policy and terms of any external website before providing personal information or using its services.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect personal information that is voluntarily submitted through this website. However, no method of electronic transmission, internet communication, or digital storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For that reason, visitors should use reasonable care when deciding what personal information to include in an online message. Every reasonable effort will be made to treat information received through this website with care and respect.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under the age of thirteen and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under thirteen.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that a child has submitted personal information through this website without appropriate permission, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, when appropriate, removed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as website practices change, new features are introduced, or legal and privacy requirements develop.

When changes are made, the revised Privacy Policy will be published on this page. The updated policy will become effective when it is posted unless a different effective date is stated.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, how information is handled, or information you have submitted through this website, please use the Contact page to reach out.

YOUR PRIVACY

Respect, Care, and Transparency

The purpose of this website is to share the message of Changed and provide readers with information and resources connected to the book. When you choose to communicate through this website, your information should be treated with the same respect and care given to every person who visits these pages.

Thank you for visiting the official website of Changed by Bradley Hobbs.
