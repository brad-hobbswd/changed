PRIVACY POLICY

Your Privacy Matters

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected while visiting the official website for Changed by Bradley Hobbs.

Information We Collect

This website is designed primarily to provide information about Changed and related resources. Personal information is collected only when voluntarily submitted through the contact form or other direct communication.

Examples may include your name, email address, and any information you choose to provide in your message.

How Information Is Used

Information submitted through this website is used solely for responding to inquiries, communicating with visitors, and improving the overall experience of the website.

Personal information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use standard browser technologies or third party hosting services that automatically collect anonymous technical information, such as browser type, device information, or pages visited.

These statistics help improve website performance and user experience without identifying individual visitors.

Third Party Services

This website may include links to third party websites for additional resources, services, or information. Once you leave this website, the privacy practices of those websites are governed by their own policies. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any external websites they choose to visit.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information submitted through this website. While no method of electronic transmission or internet storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, every effort is made to maintain appropriate safeguards for any information voluntarily provided.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed toward children under the age of thirteen and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If information is believed to have been submitted by a child without parental consent, reasonable steps will be taken to remove that information upon request.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in website practices, legal requirements, or improvements to visitor privacy. Any revisions will become effective immediately upon publication on this page.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information submitted through this website may be directed through the Contact page.

YOUR PRIVACY

Committed to Respect and Transparency

This website exists to encourage readers through the message of Changed. Personal information provided by visitors is treated with respect and used only for its intended purpose.
