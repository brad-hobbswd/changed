Purpose of This Book

Changed was written to encourage readers in their relationship with Jesus Christ through the study of God's Word. Its purpose is to strengthen faith, encourage spiritual growth, and point every reader toward a deeper walk with the Lord.

The reflections, illustrations, and personal observations contained in this book represent the author's understanding of Scripture and are offered for Christian encouragement, biblical study, and personal spiritual growth.

Readers are encouraged to search the Scriptures diligently, allowing the Word of God to remain the final authority in all matters of faith and practice.
