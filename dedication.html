DEDICATION

To My Lord and Savior

Every journey begins with grace. Before these pages were ever written, God was faithfully writing His story upon my heart.

"Every page that follows exists because of the grace of Jesus Christ."

To my Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ,

Thank You for Your mercy when I failed, Your patience when I wandered, and Your unfailing love that never let me go. Every page of this book is a testimony to Your grace and to the transforming power of Your presence. Without You, there would be no story to tell, no message worth sharing, and no life worth writing about.

To my family, thank you for your love, encouragement, and the countless ways you have walked beside me throughout this journey. Your prayers, support, and faithfulness have been a continual source of strength, and I am deeply grateful for each of you.

I would also like to express my sincere appreciation to Christian recording artist Micah Tyler. While this book is entirely original and written from my own testimony, study of Scripture, and personal experiences, the title and central theme were inspired by his song Different. That song challenged me to think deeply about what it truly means for Jesus Christ to transform a person's life. As I reflected upon its message and sought the Lord in prayer, a different question began to take shape within my own heart.

It was no longer,

"Am I different?"

It became...

Have I truly been changed?

That question stayed with me long after the music ended. It followed me through prayer, through Scripture, and through countless moments of quiet reflection before the Lord. As He continued shaping my heart, that single question became the foundation upon which every chapter of this book was written.

Changed was never intended to be merely another Christian book. It is a personal invitation to examine our hearts honestly before God and ask whether His grace has truly transformed us from the inside out.

MY PRAYER

For Every Reader

I pray these pages encourage you to seek Jesus Christ above everything else.

May His Word become your foundation. May His grace become your strength. May His presence become your greatest treasure.

And may your life become a testimony of His transforming power.

Finally, I dedicate this book to every person who has ever felt different, misunderstood, overlooked, rejected, or alone. My prayer is that these pages remind you that your identity is never found in the opinions of people but in Jesus Christ. He does not simply make us different. Through His grace, He makes us new.

May every reader come to know the life changing power of Jesus Christ and leave these pages with a deeper desire to follow Him faithfully. Whether these words encourage a lifelong believer, strengthen someone who has recently come to faith, or help a searching heart discover the Savior for the very first time, my prayer remains the same. May Jesus Christ be lifted up, His Word treasured, and His name glorified.
