DEDICATION

To My Lord and Savior

This book begins with the One who began the work in me. Before I ever understood what it meant to be changed, Jesus was already patiently teaching me who I am in Him and what it means to surrender my life completely to Him.

"Every page that follows exists because of the grace of Jesus Christ."

To my Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ,

Thank You for Your mercy when I failed, Your patience when I wandered, and Your unfailing love when I struggled to understand what You were doing in my life. Thank You for remaining faithful through every question, every disappointment, every mistake, every moment of weakness, and every moment when I felt as though no one truly understood me. Through all of it, You never stopped pursuing me, teaching me, correcting me, and reminding me that Your grace was greater than my failures.

Every page of this book exists because You have continued to work in me. I know that I am not perfect, and I know there are still areas of my life that You are changing. Yet I have learned that being changed does not mean becoming someone else. It means allowing You to transform my heart, my character, my choices, and the way I live while learning to surrender every part of who You created me to be.

I have spent much of my life knowing what it feels like to be different. As an autistic person, I know what it feels like to process the world differently, communicate differently, experience certain things differently, and sometimes be misunderstood because of those differences. There have been moments when I wondered whether being different meant that something was wrong with me or whether I needed to become more like everyone else in order to be accepted.

But You have continued to teach me something deeper. Being different does not mean that I am broken, forgotten, or less valuable. My autism is part of my story, but it is not the entirety of my identity. You know every part of me completely, including the ways I think, process, communicate, struggle, love, and experience the world. You created me, You know me, and You are not surprised by any part of me.

You do not ask me to become somebody else so that I can belong to You. Instead, You ask me to surrender myself to You. You call me to allow You to change the things within me that need to change while teaching me to trust You with every part of the person You created me to be.

That distinction became one of the reasons this book needed to be written.

I would also like to express my sincere appreciation to Christian recording artist Micah Tyler. His song Different became an important source of inspiration as I began thinking more deeply about what it means for Jesus Christ to transform a person's life. The message of that song caused me to look beyond the simple idea of being different and examine something much deeper within myself. I began asking whether I was truly allowing Jesus to change me or whether I was simply talking about change.

As I prayed and reflected on that question, something began to change in the way I understood the word different. I realized that the most important question was not whether other people could see that I was different. The more important question was whether Jesus was changing me from the inside out.

It was no longer simply,

"Am I different?"

The question became much deeper.

"Have I truly been changed?"

That question stayed with me long after the music ended. It followed me into prayer, into Scripture, and into the quiet moments when I had to be honest with myself before God. It challenged me to look beyond what I said I believed and examine whether my life actually reflected what I claimed to believe about Jesus.

I began to understand that transformation is not about creating an appearance of holiness or learning how to say the right things. It is about allowing Jesus to reach the places within us that we would rather keep hidden. It is about surrendering our pride, our fears, our failures, our habits, our attitudes, our plans, our pain, and even our desire to always be understood. It is about trusting Jesus enough to place our entire lives into His hands.

That is where Changed begins.

This book is not a story about becoming perfect, because I am not perfect and I never will be. It is not a story about pretending to have everything figured out, because I am still learning, still growing, and still being corrected by the Lord. It is also not a story about becoming someone I am not. Instead, it is the story of learning what it means to surrender myself completely to Jesus Christ and allow Him to continue the work He began in me.

There are things about me that make me different, and I no longer believe that those differences make me less worthy of God's love or less capable of serving His purpose. At the same time, being different does not place me above the need for transformation. Like every other person who follows Jesus, I have areas of my life that must be surrendered, corrected, and continually brought under His authority.

Jesus is not asking me to erase my identity. He is asking me to give Him my life.

MY PRAYER

For Every Reader

I pray these pages encourage you to seek Jesus Christ above everything else. If you have ever felt different, I pray you discover that being different does not mean that you have been forgotten. If you have ever been misunderstood, I pray you remember that Jesus understands you completely. If you have ever been rejected, I pray you discover that your worth is not determined by the opinions of people. If you have ever failed, I pray you discover the mercy that is found in Jesus Christ.

If you have ever wondered whether you can truly change, I pray you discover that transformation begins when you surrender yourself to the One who already knows you completely. May His Word become your foundation, may His grace become your strength, and may His presence become your greatest treasure.

Most of all, I pray that your life becomes a testimony of His transforming power. May you never confuse perfection with transformation, because being changed by Jesus does not mean that you will never struggle again. It means that you no longer have to face those struggles without Him.

I dedicate this book to every person who has ever felt different, misunderstood, overlooked, rejected, or alone. I especially dedicate it to those who have spent years believing that they needed to become someone else simply to be accepted. I know what that feeling can be like, and I want these pages to remind you that your value does not come from how well you fit into someone else's expectations.

You do not have to become someone else to be loved by Jesus. You do not have to pretend that your struggles do not exist, and you do not have to hide the parts of your story that make you different. At the same time, following Jesus means being willing to let Him change the things within us that keep us from becoming more like Him.

He can transform your heart without erasing your identity. He can correct your ways without destroying who you are. He can call you higher without asking you to pretend. He can take the parts of your story that others have misunderstood and use your life for His glory.

That is the hope behind these pages.

May every reader come to know Jesus Christ personally, trust Him completely, obey Him faithfully, and recognize the difference between simply saying that we have been changed and actually allowing Him to change us. Whether you have followed Jesus for many years, are just beginning to seek Him, or are still trying to understand what it means to have a relationship with Him, my prayer is that these pages lead you closer to Him.

I pray that when people encounter my life, they will not simply see my differences, my strengths, my weaknesses, my autism, my successes, or my failures. I pray that they will see evidence of the One who has been working in me all along.

When they see me, let them see Jesus.
