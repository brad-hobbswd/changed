Journey Through Changed

Every chapter builds upon the one before it, inviting the reader to look honestly at faith, identity, obedience, struggle, and the transforming work of Jesus Christ. This journey is deeply personal to me because I have experienced life through my own unique perspective, including the reality of being autistic and often experiencing the world differently from those around me. Yet this book is not about autism alone. It is about what happens when a person brings every part of who they are before Jesus Christ and allows Him to transform the heart.

My hope is that these pages help readers understand that being different does not have to become the definition of their identity. For me, autism has been part of the story, but it is not the center of the story. Jesus Christ is. The question that continues throughout this book is whether our lives truly reflect Him and whether we are willing to allow His grace to change us from the inside out.

One Question Awaits Every Reader

Before turning the next page, pause and consider the question that echoes throughout this entire book. Each of us carries a story shaped by our experiences, our struggles, our relationships, and the ways we understand the world around us. My own story includes being autistic and knowing what it feels like to experience life differently, to be misunderstood, and to search for where I belong. Yet the deepest question is not simply why we are different. The deeper question is whether we have allowed Jesus Christ to transform who we are from the inside out.

"Has Jesus truly changed me?"
