MEET THE AUTHOR

Bradley Hobbs

More than anything else, I want people to know Jesus Christ, experience His transforming grace, and discover what it truly means to be changed from the inside out.

Author • Teacher • Follower of Jesus Christ
"My greatest desire is not that people remember my name. My prayer is that they leave knowing Jesus more deeply."

Every book has a story behind it, but the greatest story is not the one found within its pages. It is the story of what Jesus Christ continues to do in the lives of ordinary people who surrender themselves completely to Him.

I have never wanted my writing to draw attention to myself. My desire has always been to point people toward the Savior who changes hearts, restores broken lives, and gives hope where none seems possible. If someone finishes reading one of my books thinking more about Jesus than they do about me, then I believe I have accomplished exactly what God called me to do.

The message of Changed did not begin with an idea for a book. It began with a question that God continued placing before my heart.

Has Jesus truly changed me?

Not simply my habits. Not simply my church attendance. Not simply the words I speak. But my heart.

That question became the foundation of this book, and in many ways it also became the foundation of my own journey with Christ. Every chapter grew out of prayer, Scripture, and the ongoing work of God's grace as He continued shaping my own life into the image of His Son.

Like many people, my journey has included seasons of joy, disappointment, misunderstanding, and growth. Looking back, I can now see that God was faithfully working through every season, even when I could not understand what He was doing.

Some of the experiences that once caused me to question His purpose eventually became the very things He used to strengthen my faith, deepen my dependence upon Him, and remind me that my identity has never been found in the opinions of people.

Throughout much of my life, I heard the same words repeated in different ways.

People often told me I was different. Sometimes they meant it kindly. Other times they did not.

I was told I cared too much. That I asked too many questions. That I was too honest. Too direct. Too detailed. Too loud. Too bright. Too intense. Too passionate. Too much.

For a long time, I wondered why I always seemed to experience the world differently than the people around me. I noticed details others overlooked. I valued honesty even when it made conversations uncomfortable. I found myself questioning compromises that everyone else seemed willing to accept without hesitation.

Living with autism has shaped the way I experience life. I often communicate directly rather than through the unspoken social filters many people naturally rely upon. I usually mean exactly what I say, and I often understand the words people speak rather than the hidden meanings they expect others to recognize. Because of that, my sincerity has sometimes been mistaken for bluntness, my enthusiasm mistaken for intensity, and my desire to be truthful misunderstood as criticism.

There were many moments when I left conversations wondering what I had done wrong. I knew my intentions were good, yet I often discovered that what I meant was not always what others heard.

For years I believed that if I could somehow become more like everyone else, life would finally make sense.

Then God changed my perspective.

He never asked me to become like everyone else.

He called me to become more like Jesus Christ.

That realization changed everything.

I stopped measuring my life by how well I fit into the expectations of other people and began measuring it by my willingness to follow Christ. The Lord helped me understand that my purpose was never to earn acceptance from the world. My purpose was to surrender my heart completely to Him and allow Him to transform me from the inside out.

God never wastes our experiences. The very things that once made me feel isolated became the very tools He used to teach me dependence, perseverance, humility, compassion, and faith. The misunderstandings taught me patience. The loneliness taught me where my identity truly belonged. The questions I carried for years eventually led me deeper into His Word, where I discovered that my value has never depended upon the opinions of people.

A LESSON GOD TAUGHT ME

Being Different Was Never The Goal

For many years I believed my story was about being different. God showed me it was always about being changed.

Jesus Christ did not come to make me fit into the world. He came to transform my heart and make me more like Himself.

That truth became the heartbeat of Changed. The book is not about autism. It is not about personality. It is not about trying to convince people that being different is somehow special.

It is about the transforming power of Jesus Christ. It is about what happens when a person truly surrenders every part of life to Him. Because when Jesus changes a heart, He changes a life.

WHY I WRITE

More Than Writing A Book

Writing has never been about publishing a book or adding another title to my name. Long before there were chapters, there was prayer. Long before there was a manuscript, there was Scripture. God began shaping my heart long before He ever placed a message in my hands.

The inspiration for Changed came after hearing the song Different by Christian recording artist Micah Tyler. As I reflected on its message and spent time in prayer, another word continued to return to my heart.

Changed.

That single word became more than a title. It became a question.

Has Jesus truly changed me?

Not simply my behavior. Not simply my knowledge of Scripture. Not simply the image people see. But the person I am when no one else is watching.

That question became the foundation for every chapter of this book. Each page was written with one purpose: to encourage readers to move beyond religion and discover the transforming power of a genuine relationship with Jesus Christ.

My desire has never been to write books that simply provide information. I want to write books that invite people to examine their hearts in the light of God's Word, grow in their faith, and walk more closely with Jesus.

My Prayer For Every Reader

I do not hope people finish this book impressed by the writing.

I hope they finish loving Jesus more deeply.

I do not hope they remember my story.

I hope they remember His grace.

I do not hope they leave talking about the author.

I hope they leave talking about the Savior.

Whether someone has followed Christ for many years, recently placed their faith in Him, or is still searching for answers, my prayer is exactly the same.

May these pages remind them that Jesus Christ is still changing lives today. He is still restoring what has been broken. He is still giving hope to the hopeless. He is still making all things new.
