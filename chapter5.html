CHAPTER FIVE

The Evidence of a Changed Life

Long before anyone examines a tree's roots, they can look at its fruit and know what kind of tree it is.

"The strongest evidence that Jesus has changed your life is not what you claim with your lips, but what is consistently revealed through your life."

Every tree tells a story.

Long before anyone examines its roots, they can look at its fruit and know what kind of tree it is. An apple tree does not struggle to produce oranges, nor does an orange tree suddenly begin bearing apples. The fruit simply reveals the nature of the tree.

Jesus used this simple illustration to teach a profound spiritual truth. A changed heart will eventually produce a changed life. The transformation that God begins within us cannot remain hidden forever because what fills the heart eventually shapes the way we speak, the way we think, and the way we live.

SCRIPTURE

"Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit."

Matthew 7:17

This is why Jesus said that a good tree bears good fruit. He was not teaching that people earn salvation through good works. He was teaching that a life transformed by God naturally begins to reflect the character of the One who transformed it.

Fruit is never the root of salvation. It is the evidence of salvation.

That distinction is important because many people spend their lives trying to produce spiritual fruit without first surrendering their hearts to Christ. They become exhausted trying to maintain an image of holiness while neglecting the relationship that makes holiness possible. Christianity is not about attaching fruit to a dead tree. It is about God giving life to what was once spiritually dead.

When Jesus changes a person, He does not simply alter outward behavior. He changes the desires that drive that behavior. A heart that once loved sin begins to hate it. A heart that once lived for self begins to find joy in serving others. A heart that once pursued the approval of people begins to long for the approval of God.

These changes do not happen because we possess extraordinary strength. They happen because the Holy Spirit faithfully produces His fruit within those who belong to Christ.

SCRIPTURE

"But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness, temperance..."

Galatians 5:22–23

Love begins replacing hatred. Joy begins overcoming despair. Peace steadies the heart in the middle of uncertainty. Patience grows where frustration once ruled. Kindness becomes a natural response instead of an occasional effort. Goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, and self control gradually become visible because Christ is continuing His work within us.

None of these qualities are manufactured through determination alone. They grow as we remain connected to the One who is the source of life.

REFLECTION

What Kind Of Fruit Are You Producing?

A changed heart will always reveal itself through a changed life. The fruit may grow slowly, but it will grow because Christ is faithfully at work within those who belong to Him.

There have been seasons in my own life when I wanted people to understand my intentions more than I wanted them to see Christ in me. I wanted misunderstandings to disappear and for every false assumption to be corrected. Over time, the Lord began asking me a different question.

"What kind of fruit are you producing while you are waiting to be understood?"

That question humbled me. It reminded me that my responsibility is not to control how people perceive me. My responsibility is to remain faithful to Christ regardless of whether my faithfulness is recognized by others.

Sometimes the greatest testimony is not winning an argument, but responding with grace when you have every reason to become angry, choosing forgiveness when resentment seems justified, and continuing to love people who misunderstand you because Christ has never stopped loving you.

That is the kind of fruit that cannot be manufactured. It grows only in a heart that has been changed by God.

The world often celebrates talent, influence, and success, but Heaven measures something entirely different. God is not impressed by the size of our platform if our character does not reflect His Son. He is far more concerned with who we are becoming than with how many people know our names.

This truth has challenged me many times. There have been moments when I wanted God to change my circumstances before changing me. I prayed for difficult situations to disappear, only to discover that He was using those very situations to produce patience, humility, perseverance, and compassion within my heart.

Looking back, I would not trade those lessons. Fruit grows slowly. It develops through seasons of sunshine and seasons of rain, and strong roots are often formed in places where no one else can see them.

The same is true in the Christian life. There are moments when we feel as though God is silent, yet beneath the surface He is strengthening our faith. There are seasons when prayers seem unanswered, yet He is teaching us to trust His timing instead of our own. There are trials that leave us asking why, only to discover later that they produced a deeper dependence upon Him than comfort ever could have achieved.

SCRIPTURE

"Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me."

John 15:4

The changed life is not measured by dramatic moments alone. It is measured by daily faithfulness and revealed through the choices we make each day. It is seen in telling the truth when dishonesty would be easier, choosing integrity when no one else is watching, responding with humility when pride demands recognition, and extending compassion to those who may never return our kindness.

These ordinary acts of obedience become extraordinary because they reveal the life of Christ within us.

Faithfulness Reveals The Fruit

Perhaps as you read this chapter, you find yourself discouraged because your growth seems slower than you expected.

Do not lose heart.

Fruit does not appear overnight. A healthy tree grows one season at a time, and the believer grows in much the same way. God is patient with His children because He knows the work He has begun within them. He is not rushing the process, nor has He abandoned it. Continue abiding in Christ, seeking His presence, obeying His Word, and trusting His promises, knowing that He is faithfully shaping you into the person He has called you to become.

The evidence of His work will become visible, not because you are striving to impress others, but because the life of Jesus is steadily transforming your own.

One day, someone may look at your life and wonder what made the difference.

Your answer will not be your intelligence.

It will not be your determination or your personality.

It will be Jesus Christ.

When He changes the root, the fruit cannot help but change as well. That is the unmistakable evidence of a life that has truly been changed.

FINAL THOUGHT

Changed From The Inside Out

God is not looking for people who appear righteous on the outside while remaining unchanged within. He is transforming hearts, and changed hearts always produce changed lives. Continue abiding in Christ, trusting His work, and allowing His Spirit to produce fruit that brings glory to Him.

SCRIPTURE

"And being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ."

Philippians 1:6
