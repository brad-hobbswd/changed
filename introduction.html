INTRODUCTION

Does My Life Truly Reflect Jesus Christ?

Every transformed life begins with one honest question. Before we explore what it means to be changed, we must first ask whether our lives truly reflect Jesus Christ.

"Does my life truly reflect Jesus Christ?"

Some questions deserve more than a quick answer because they require us to honestly examine our lives in the light of God's Word.

One of those questions is this.

Does my life truly reflect Jesus Christ?

That question is not about church attendance, biblical knowledge, or the words we speak on Sunday. It is about the condition of our hearts. If someone watched your life every day, would they see evidence that Jesus Christ has truly changed you?

The world teaches that change comes through determination, self improvement, or becoming a better version of yourself. The gospel tells a completely different story. Jesus Christ did not come to make bad people a little better. He came to forgive sin, restore what was broken, and make spiritually dead people alive.

The greatest miracle is not a changed circumstance.

It is a changed heart.

When Jesus Christ changes your heart, everything else begins to change with it. Your priorities begin to change. Your choices begin to change. Even your desires begin to change because you are no longer living for yourself. The things that once satisfied you lose their hold, and the approval of people matters far less than the approval of God.

There have been many times in my own life when I felt like I did not fit in. I often wondered why compromise seemed so easy for others or why truth was exchanged for convenience. Over time, God taught me that He never promised His followers would always be accepted by the world. He promised that He would never leave them, and that promise is far greater.

Jesus Christ was misunderstood, rejected, and opposed because He stood for truth. If we are becoming more like Him, we should not be surprised when we experience the same struggles.

THE INVITATION

This book is not an invitation to be different for the sake of being different.

It is an invitation to surrender your life completely to Jesus Christ and allow Him to transform you from the inside out.

As you read these pages, my prayer is that you will be challenged, encouraged, and drawn closer to the One who is still changing lives today. No matter where you have been or what your past looks like, Jesus Christ is still restoring hearts and making all things new.

Before you turn the next page,

Ask yourself one simple question.

Has Jesus truly changed me?

If your answer is yes, I pray this book strengthens your walk with Him. If your answer is no, I pray you encounter the One who can change your life forever.
