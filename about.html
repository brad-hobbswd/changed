ABOUT THE BOOK

Every Journey Begins With A Question

Has Jesus truly changed me? That question became the heartbeat of Changed and the reason this book was written.

"Jesus did not come to make us a better version of ourselves. He came to make us new."

Changed was written to encourage readers to move beyond outward religion and discover the transforming power of a genuine relationship with Jesus Christ. More than a book about change, it is an invitation to examine the heart in the light of God's Word and ask whether Jesus has truly made us new.

Every chapter points toward one central truth. Christianity is not primarily about becoming a better version of ourselves. It is about surrendering our lives to Jesus Christ and allowing Him to transform us from the inside out.

Why This Book Was Written

Every believer reaches moments when faith becomes deeply personal. There comes a time when we must look beyond appearances, traditions, and routines and honestly ask whether our lives truly reflect Jesus Christ. That question became the beginning of this book.

For years, I believed many of life's greatest struggles centered around feeling different. Through prayer, the study of Scripture, and God's faithful work in my life, I began to understand something far greater.

The greatest need was never to become different.

The greatest need was to become more like Jesus Christ.

That realization reshaped the way I viewed my own journey. My identity was never meant to be found in the opinions of others, nor in the circumstances I faced. My identity has always been found in Christ. From that truth, Changed began to take shape.

WHAT THIS BOOK IS

A Journey Of Transformation

This book is an invitation to examine the heart through the light of God's Word.

It explores what happens when Jesus Christ transforms a life from the inside out.

Each chapter encourages readers to grow in faith, obedience, and a deeper relationship with the Savior.

This is not a book about self improvement or positive thinking. It is not a guide to becoming more successful, more accepted, or more admired. It is about Jesus Christ and the transforming work that only He can accomplish within the human heart.

Whether you have followed Christ for many years, have recently begun your walk with Him, or are still searching for answers, my prayer is that these pages will encourage you to draw closer to the One who still changes lives today.

A NEW CREATION

"Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new."

2 Corinthians 5:17

One Question Remains

As you prepare to begin this journey, I encourage you to pause for a moment and honestly examine your own heart. Every chapter that follows points back to one question that only you can answer.

Has Jesus truly changed me?

Not simply my habits. Not simply my words. Not simply my church attendance. But my heart.

If that question leads you to know Jesus Christ more deeply, trust Him more completely, and surrender your life more fully to His grace, then this book has fulfilled the purpose for which it was written.

MY PRAYER

More Than Finishing A Book

My hope is not that you simply finish another Christian book.

My prayer is that you encounter Jesus Christ in a deeper way than ever before.

Because the greatest story any of us will ever tell is not how we changed ourselves.

It is how Jesus Christ changed us.
