ABOUT THE BOOK

Every Journey Begins With A Question

Has Jesus truly changed me? That question became the heartbeat of Changed and the reason this book was written.

More Than Being Different

After hearing the Christian song Different by Micah Tyler, I found myself reflecting on what genuine transformation through Jesus Christ truly looks like. The song planted a question within my heart, but through prayer and Scripture God led me to something even deeper.

Jesus never came to make us different. He came to make us new.

That truth became the foundation of this book. Changed was born from a desire to understand what it truly means to surrender completely to Jesus Christ and allow Him to transform every part of our lives from the inside out.

WHY I WROTE CHANGED

A Personal Journey

Throughout much of my life, I have experienced the world differently. Living with autism has often meant processing conversations, relationships, and everyday life in ways that sometimes left me feeling misunderstood or out of place. I frequently wondered why I seemed to value honesty, truth, and consistency while compromise often appeared normal to those around me.

For years I believed my greatest struggle was feeling different. God showed me that my greatest need was not to become like everyone else. My greatest need was to become more like Jesus Christ.

That realization changed everything. I began to understand that my identity was never meant to be found in the opinions of people or even in the way I experienced the world. My identity has always been found in Jesus Christ.

The experiences that once made me question where I belonged became the very experiences God used to teach me about His grace, His faithfulness, and His transforming power. This book is the result of that journey.

01

What This Book Is

Changed is a gospel centered journey through biblical transformation. It explores what happens when Jesus Christ changes a heart and how that inward work becomes visible through everyday faith, obedience, and spiritual growth.

02

What This Book Is Not

This book is not about autism, personality, or trying to be unique. It is not a guide to self improvement or positive thinking. It is about Jesus Christ and the transforming work only He can accomplish within the human heart.

03

The Invitation

Every chapter invites readers to move beyond outward religion and honestly examine whether Jesus Christ has truly transformed their lives from the inside out.

WHO THIS BOOK IS FOR

Written For Every Heart Seeking Christ

Whether you have followed Jesus for many years, recently began your walk with Him, or are still searching for answers, this book was written to encourage you toward a deeper relationship with the Savior.

If you have ever wrestled with questions about your identity, your purpose, your faith, or whether genuine transformation is truly possible, my prayer is that these pages will point you beyond yourself and toward Jesus Christ.

The goal has never been to become different. The goal has always been to become more like Jesus.

THE HEART OF CHANGED

One Question Remains

Every chapter ultimately returns to one question that only you can answer.

Has Jesus Truly Changed Me?

Not simply my habits. Not simply my words. Not simply my church attendance. But my heart.

If this question leads you to know Jesus more deeply, trust Him more completely, and surrender your life more fully to His grace, then this book has fulfilled the purpose for which it was written.

A FINAL THOUGHT

My Prayer For Every Reader

My hope is not that you simply finish another Christian book. My prayer is that you encounter Jesus Christ in a deeper way than ever before.

If these pages encourage you to examine your heart honestly, trust God's Word more completely, and surrender your life more fully to Christ, then this book has accomplished exactly what I prayed it would.

Because the greatest story any of us will ever tell is not how we changed ourselves. It is how Jesus Christ changed us.

"When Jesus changes the root, the fruit cannot help but change."
