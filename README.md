# Changed

> **A Journey of Faith. A Life Transformed. A Purpose Revealed.**

**Changed** is the official companion website for the Christian book *Changed* by **Bradley Hobbs**. Inspired by the message behind Micah Tyler's song *Different*, this project explores the transforming power of Jesus Christ and what it truly means to live a life changed from the inside out.

🌐 **Live Website**

https://brad-hobbswd.github.io/changed

---

## About the Book

Following Jesus is not about becoming a better version of yourself.

It is about becoming a new creation.

*Changed* invites readers to examine their hearts through Scripture, personal testimony, and biblical reflection, discovering that authentic Christianity is measured not merely by what we profess, but by the transformation Christ produces within us.

> *"Being changed does not end the battle with the flesh. It changes who is leading the battle."*
