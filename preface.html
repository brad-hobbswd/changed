PREFACE

The Journey Behind Changed

Before you begin reading, I want to share why this book was written and what Jesus has been teaching me about difference, identity, surrender, obedience, and genuine transformation.

5 Minute Read • The Story Behind The Book

There are moments in life that change everything, not necessarily because our circumstances suddenly become easier, but because our understanding of God becomes deeper. Sometimes the greatest change does not happen around us at all. It happens within us when we finally become willing to let Jesus speak into the places of our hearts that we have spent years protecting, avoiding, or trying to understand on our own.

For much of my life, I have known what it feels like to be different. As an autistic person, I have often experienced the world in ways that other people did not always understand. I have had to process conversations, situations, expectations, and relationships differently, and there have been times when I wondered why something that seemed simple to someone else could feel so complicated to me. There were also seasons when being misunderstood left me feeling isolated, overlooked, or as though I needed to change who I was simply to belong.

Those experiences shaped me, but they also taught me something important. Being different is not the same thing as being broken, and being misunderstood does not mean that God does not understand me. Jesus has always known me completely. He knows the way I think, the way I process life, the things that overwhelm me, the things that bring me joy, the ways I communicate, and the places where I struggle. Nothing about me is hidden from Him.

At the same time, I have learned that knowing God accepts me does not mean that I no longer need to grow. My autism does not remove my responsibility to follow Jesus, and being different does not give me permission to remain unchanged in the areas where He is calling me to surrender. There are parts of my character, my choices, my attitudes, and my responses that Jesus has had to confront. There are things I have had to learn to surrender because following Him requires more than simply knowing who He is.

Jesus never called me

to become someone else.

He called me to become more like Him.

That truth reshaped the way I began to understand my own life. I realized that Jesus was not asking me to erase the things that make me who I am. He was asking me to surrender the things within me that were keeping me from becoming more obedient, more loving, more truthful, more forgiving, more faithful, and more dependent upon Him.

That is an important distinction because Christianity is not about creating a version of ourselves that looks acceptable to other people. It is not about learning how to say the right words while our hearts remain untouched. It is not about appearing changed while continuing to live according to our own desires. Genuine transformation reaches deeper than appearance. It changes the way we think, the way we respond, the way we treat people, the way we handle failure, the way we forgive, the way we trust, and ultimately the way we surrender our lives to Jesus Christ.

"When Jesus changes the heart, the life begins to change."

That truth is simple, but living it is not always easy. There have been many moments when I have wanted Jesus to change my circumstances while resisting the changes He was asking me to make within myself. I have wanted Him to remove the struggle without always wanting Him to teach me through it. I have wanted answers without always wanting to wait. I have wanted to be understood without always being willing to understand others. I have wanted to say that I trusted Him while still trying to control the outcome.

Those contradictions are part of why this book had to be written.

THE HEART OF THIS BOOK

More Than Being Different

Changed is not about autism, personality, individuality, or trying to become unique. It is about what happens when a person becomes willing to surrender every part of life to Jesus Christ and allow Him to transform the heart.

I want to be honest with you from the beginning. I am not writing this book because I have mastered everything I am about to discuss. I am writing because I am still learning. I am still growing. I still make mistakes, still have questions, and still encounter areas of my life where Jesus is asking me to trust Him more deeply. The difference is that I no longer want to hide those places from Him.

I want Jesus to have all of me.

I want Him to have the parts of my life that are easy to surrender and the parts that are much harder. I want Him to have my plans, my expectations, my disappointments, my fears, my relationships, my weaknesses, my strengths, my failures, and my future. I want to stop measuring my faith by what I say and start allowing my life to demonstrate what I truly believe.

That is the journey you are about to begin.

The eight chapters that follow are not meant to provide a formula for becoming a perfect Christian. Instead, they are intended to take us deeper into the process of surrender. We will examine what it means to recognize our need for change, listen to Jesus, see ourselves through His eyes, surrender what we have been holding onto, confront unhealthy patterns, remember what the cross says about our worth, allow our lives to become evidence of His work, and ultimately give Jesus permission to continue what He started in us.

Along the way, I hope you will find places that challenge you, encourage you, and perhaps even make you uncomfortable. I believe there are times when discomfort is necessary because Jesus loves us too much to leave us exactly where we are. His grace does not simply make us feel better about ourselves. His grace invites us into a deeper relationship with Him and gives us the strength to become who He has called us to be.

Has Jesus Truly Changed Me?

This is the question I want you to carry throughout every chapter. I am not asking whether you know the right words, attend the right church, or appear to have everything together. I am asking whether Jesus has been given access to your heart and whether His presence is changing the way you live.

The question is not meant to condemn you. It is meant to invite you into honesty. If there are areas where you still need to grow, bring them to Jesus. If there are places where you have failed, bring them to Jesus. If there are parts of your story that you do not understand, bring them to Jesus. You do not have to pretend before the One who already knows you completely.

Come honestly. Come surrendered. Come willing to be changed.

My prayer is that as you move through these pages, you will not simply gain information about Christian living. I pray that you encounter Jesus more deeply. I pray that His Word challenges you, His grace strengthens you, and His presence draws you into a relationship that goes beyond religion and reaches into every part of your life.

I also pray that if you have ever felt different, misunderstood, rejected, or alone, you will discover that your differences do not place you outside the reach of God's purpose. You do not have to become somebody else to be loved by Him. You do, however, have to be willing to surrender yourself to Him, because Jesus does not simply want a portion of our lives. He calls us to give Him everything.

If you reach the final page and find yourself loving Jesus more deeply, trusting Him more completely, desiring to obey Him more faithfully, and becoming more willing to let Him change you from within, then this book will have accomplished what I prayed it would.

Welcome to the journey through Changed. Let us begin by asking the question that started it all: Has Jesus truly changed me?
